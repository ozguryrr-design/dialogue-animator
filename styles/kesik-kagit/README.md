# Kesik Kâğıt (Paper Cut)

Cut-paper puppets: no outlines, layered paper shadows, slightly trembling cut edges, characters animated on twos while the camera moves on ones.

- id: `kesik-kagit` · family: `paper` · body: `paper-cut`
- palette (7 colours): `#F3EBDD` `#22304A` `#9DB89A` `#F2B8A2` `#D9653B` `#2F7F7A` `#E3A72F`
- motion: characters at 15 fps, push-in 0.12 %/frame, pan ≤ 18 px/frame
- transitions: cut = cut, scene = paper-slide (10 f)
- captions font: Baloo 2 700
